Privacy Policy

BMI - FitAssist (Android package com.giftsonx.bmifitassist)
Effective date: July 21, 2026

BMI - FitAssist is a fully offline body-mass-index (BMI) calculator. This policy explains, plainly, what the app does and does not do with your information.

Summary

BMI - FitAssist does not collect, transmit, share, or sell any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Information the app stores on your device

To make the app convenient to reuse, the following values are saved in the app's local storage on your device only:

  • The height you enter
  • The weight you enter
  • Your preferred measurement units (metric or imperial)

This information never leaves your device. The app has no backend server, makes no network requests, and works entirely offline.

Information the app does not collect

  • No accounts, sign-in, or registration
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No location data
  • No contacts, photos, files, or other device data
  • No cookies or third-party SDKs that collect data
  • No data shared with any third party

Data deletion

Because all data is stored locally, you are in full control. To delete it, clear the app's storage in your device settings (Settings → Apps → BMI - FitAssist → Storage → Clear data) or simply uninstall the app. No copy exists anywhere else.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone — it collects no information at all.

Not medical advice

BMI - FitAssist is a general wellness tool. BMI is a simple screening measure and the app's output is for informational purposes only. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for questions about your health.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices ever change, this policy will be updated and the effective date above revised. Because the app collects nothing, changes are unlikely.
